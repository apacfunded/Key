How it works

1. Pick a prefix. Your ticker, a word, your initials. 1 to 5 characters.

2. Your browser grinds the key. It tries millions of random mint keys until one starts with your prefix. The key never leaves your device.

3. Launch on pump.fun. You approve once in your wallet and the coin goes live with your custom CA.

Your keys

Nothing leaves your device.

  • Made in your browser. The mint key is ground on your device and never sent to Key.
  • Used once. It signs the pump.fun create transaction, then it is thrown away.

Questions

Is it still a pump.fun coin?

Yes. Key only chooses the mint address. Your coin is created on pump.fun and trades there like any other: same bonding curve, same migration.

Who holds the mint key?

Only you, and only for a moment. It's generated in your browser, used once to sign the pump.fun create transaction, then thrown away. It's never sent to Key. pump.fun removes mint and freeze authority at launch, so the key has no power over your coin afterward.

What does it cost?

There is no launch fee. You pay pump.fun's normal costs, Solana network fees and any dev buy you choose. After launch, 20% of your coin's creator rewards buys back and burns $KEY, and you keep 80%.

Where does the 20% go?

To the buyback wallet. It is used to buy $KEY off the market, and the $KEY it buys is burned. Key keeps none of it.

Why do some prefixes take longer?

Each extra character is about 58× more work. The first letter matters too: Solana addresses start with some letters far more often than others, so the launch page shows the real odds for your exact prefix.

Which characters can I use?

Digits 1–9 and letters, except 0, capital O, capital I and lowercase l. Matching is case-sensitive unless you turn on "any case", which is much faster.
